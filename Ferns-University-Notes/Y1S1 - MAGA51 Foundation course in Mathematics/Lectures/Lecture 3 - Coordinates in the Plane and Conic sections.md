### 4.
Decide the centre and the halfaxles of the elipsis $3x^{2}-6x+2y^{2}+4y+4=0$
$$
\begin{align}
3x^{2}-6x+2y^{2}+4y+4=0 \\
 \\
2y^{2}+4y\iff 2(y^{2}+2y) \iff 2(y^{2}+2y+1-1) \iff 2((y+1)^{2}-1)\iff 2(y+1)^{2}-2 \\
 \\
3x^{2}-6x\iff 3(x^{2}-2x)\iff 3(x^{2}-2x+1-1)\iff 3((x-1)^{2}-1) \\
3(x-1)^{2}-3 \\
 \\
3x^{2}-6x+y^{2}+4y+4=0\iff 3(x-1)^{2}-3+2(y+1)^{2}-2+4=0 \\
3(x-1)^{2}+2(y+1)^{2}=1 \iff \dfrac{(x-1)^{2}}{\dfrac{1}{3}}+\dfrac{(y+1)^{2}}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=1 \\
\dfrac{(x-1)^{2}}{\left( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{ 3 }} \right)^{2} }+\dfrac{(y+1)^{2}}{\left( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }} \right)^{2}}=1
\end{align}
$$
Conclusion
The elipsis is centered in $(1,-1)$, with the halfaxis $x$ being $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{ 3 }}$ and the half axis $y$ being $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{ 2 }}$

### 5.
Give the asymptotes for the hyperbola $6x^{2}-12x-y^{2}+4y+1=0$
$$
\begin{align}
6x^{2}-12x-y^{2}+4y+1=0 \\
 \\
\text{Square completion of }x \\
6x^{2}-12x=6(x^{2}-2x)=6(x^{2}-2x+1-1)=6((x-1)^{2}-1)=6(x-1)^{2}-6 \\
 \\
\text{Square completion of }y \\
-y^{2}+4y=-1(y^{2}-4y)=-1(y^{2}-4y+4-4)=-1((y-2)^{2}-4)=-1(y-2)^{2}+4 \\
 \\
6(x-1)^{2}-6+-1(y-2)^{2}+4+1=0 \iff 6(x-1)^{2}-6-(y-2)^{2}+4+1=0  \\
6(x-1)^{2}-(y-2)^{2}=1 \iff \dfrac{(x-1)^{2}}{\dfrac{1}{6}}-\dfrac{(y-2)^{2}}{1}=1 \\
\dfrac{(x-1)^{2}}{\left( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{ 6 }} \right)^{2} }-\dfrac{(y-2)^{2}}{1^{2}}=1
\end{align}
$$
hyperbola equation obtained!
asymptote 1:
$$
\dfrac{x-1}{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{ 6 }}}+\dfrac{y-2}{1}=0
$$
asymptote 2:
$$
