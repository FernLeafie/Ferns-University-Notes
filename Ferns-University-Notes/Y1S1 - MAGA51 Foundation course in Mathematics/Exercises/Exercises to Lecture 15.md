## Examples
### 1

$$
\begin{align}
\cos x>1-\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}, x>0 \\
\cos x-1+\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}>0 \\
 \\
-\sin x+x>0 \\
x-\sin x>0
\end{align}
$$
Since the derivative is always positive, the function is rising
### 2

$$
\begin{align}
\lim_{ x \to 0 } \dfrac{\sin 3x-3\sin x}{4e^{2x}-8x^{2}-8x-4} \overset{\text{L'H}}{=}\lim_{ x \to 0 } \dfrac{3\cos 3x+3\cos x}{8^{2x}-16x-8}=\ `` \dfrac{0}{0} " \\
\overset{\text{L'H}}{=}\lim_{ x \to 0 } \dfrac{-9\cos 3x+3\sin x}{16e^{2x}-16}=\ `` \dfrac{0}{0} " \\
\overset{\text{L'H}}{=}\lim_{ x \to 0 } \dfrac{-27\cos x+3\cos x}{32e^{2x}}=\ `` \dfrac{-27\cos x+3}{32} "
\end{align}
$$
