## Examples
#### 1
$$
\begin{align}
f(x)=x^{4}-2x^{2}+6 \\
x^{2}=t \\
t^{2}-2t+6=t^{2}-2t+1-1+6=(t-1)^{2}+5 \\
\underbrace{ (x^{2}-1)^{2} }_{ \geq 0 }+5
\end{align}
$$
min is 5, when $x^{2}-1=0\implies x=\pm1$
#### 2

$$
\begin{align}
9^{x}-5*3^{x}-3^{1-x}+7=0\iff 3^{2x}-5*3^{x}-\dfrac{3}{3^{x}}+7=0 \\
t=3^{x} \\
t^{2}-5t-\dfrac{3}{t}+7=0 \iff t^{3}-5t^{2}+7t-3=0\iff (t-1)(t^{2}-4t+3)=0 \\
(t-1)(t-1)(t-3)=0\iff \left\{ \begin{array}{lll}
t_{1}=1 \\
t_{2}=1 \\
t_{3}=3
\end{array} \right.  \\
\left\{ \begin{array}{lll}
3^{x}=3 \\
3^{x}=1
\end{array} \right. \iff \left\{ \begin{array}{lll}
x=1 \\
x=0
\end{array} \right. 
\end{align}
$$
#### 3

$$
\begin{align}
2\ln\left( \dfrac{1}{x-1} \right)+\ln(x+2)=\ln 2\iff -2\ln(x-1)+\ln(x+2)=\ln2 \\
\ln (x+2)=\ln 2+ 2\ln(x-1) \iff \ln(x+2)=\ln2+\ln(x-1)^{2} \\
\ln(x+2)=\ln(2*(x-1)^{2}) \iff x+2=2(x-1)^{2}
\end{align}
$$
