$x^{2}\sqrt{ y(x) }+\dfrac{y(x)}{x}=2$ for $(1,1)$
$$
\begin{align}

(1,1) \in G_{y} \\
 \\
\left( x^{2}\sqrt{ y(x) }+\dfrac{y(x)}{x} \right)'=(2)' \\
2x\sqrt{ y }+x^{2} \dfrac{1}{2}y^{-1/2}y' + \dfrac{y'*x-y*1}{x^{2}}=0 \\
2+1 \dfrac{1}{2}1y' + \dfrac{y'*1-1*1}{1}=0 \\
\dfrac{y'}{2}+\dfrac{y'}{2}=1 \\
y'=1
\end{align}
$$
